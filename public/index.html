Why this exists

Somewhere between the standup and the steering committee, English quietly left the building. Nobody actually decided to leverage synergies or circle back offline — it just accretes, meeting by meeting, until a simple sentence needs a glossary.

Jargon Buster pulls a real, machine-generated corporate phrase from the wild, runs it through a small in-house translation engine, and hands you back what a person would actually say. No AI theatre, no seven-step framework — just the blunt version.

Use it before a townhall to brace yourself. Use it after one, to feel something.

Filed under

  • No logins. No accounts. No onboarding flow for a translator.
  • Nothing you generate leaves your device except when you choose to share it.
  • Runs entirely on a Cloudflare Worker — fast wherever you open it.
